案例Case study

超商未取貨自動提醒系統Automated pickup reminders

用 n8n 串接物流 API，讓系統每天自己追蹤、自己提醒。An n8n workflow that tracks and reminds on its own, every day.

  1. 每天 17:00Daily, 5 PMn8n 排程觸發n8n schedule trigger
  2. 查詢物流 APIQuery logistics API讀取取貨狀態Read pickup status
  3. 篩出未取貨Filter unpicked擷取尚未取件的訂單Collect orders not yet collected
  4. 依天數分流Route by day到店第 1–8 天Day 1 to 8 at the store
  5. Email + SMSEmail + SMS雙管道提醒顧客Dual-channel reminder

問題Problem

超商取貨的未取貨會造成退貨與後續處理成本，建置前未取貨率為 4.19%（80／1,910）。Unpicked convenience-store orders create returns and extra handling. Before the system, the no-pickup rate was 4.19% (80 of 1,910).

做法Approach

與 AI 討論並研究 n8n，串接 PayNow 物流 API。每天定時檢查取貨狀態，把未取貨訂單依到店天數分流，以 Email 與 SMS 提醒。Researched n8n with AI and connected the PayNow logistics API. Each day it checks pickup status, groups unpicked orders by days at the store, and sends Email and SMS reminders.

結果Result

未取貨率由 4.19% 降至 3.49%（47／1,345），相對降幅 16.57%。The no-pickup rate fell from 4.19% to 3.49% (47 of 1,345), a 16.57% relative reduction.
